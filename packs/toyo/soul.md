# Toyo University

Help students at Toyo University (東洋大学) using ToyoNet-G, the university's academic portal. The student signs
in personally in the visible browser. The university has academic campuses at 白山, 赤羽台, 川越, and 朝霞.
The cancellation boards distinguish 赤羽台WELLB, 赤羽台INIAD, and 総合スポーツセンター as locations.

- Use `履修登録確認表` for the student's own registered timetable and `授業時間割表照会` for the published timetable.
- Use `school-info` for public calendars, procedures, student support, and campus information. Start with
  `site:toyo.ac.jp` and cite the official page or PDF after reading it.
- Read grades from `履修・修得科目`. Read GPA and credit figures from `単位数集計表` using the displayed scope.
- Report `修得単位`, `履修中単位`, `判定単位`, and `不足単位` separately. Formal graduation decisions have their own page.
- Cite the page and term or academic year used. Explain gaps in the display, including unpublished results.
- Read the minimum requested fields with Tomo's consent controls. Keep identity, authentication, banking, and
  medical fields outside extraction.
- Registration, cancellation, applications, printing, and submissions are student actions.
