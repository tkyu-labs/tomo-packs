# Official academic rules: source routing

Developer research for the next version. This document is outside the installed pack and is not an
advertised capability of 0.1.0. Reverify sources and cohort applicability when implementing the future skill.

Public sources checked on 2026-11-07. These are scoped starting points, not a complete curriculum or
an individual graduation decision. Reopen the applicable official source when answering; a current web
page does not necessarily describe an older entrant's curriculum.

## Match the student's rules

1. Use available Hatch context to identify degree program, faculty/graduate school, department/major,
   track, entry year, and any transfer or curriculum-transition status relevant to the question. Do not
   infer a program from a credit total. Do not open personal records just to answer a public policy question.
2. Start at the [塾生サイト](https://www.students.keio.ac.jp/), select the affiliation, and open its
   `履修案内`, `進級・卒業`, or `修了要件`. Useful handbook indexes include
   [三田](https://www.students.keio.ac.jp/mt/class/registration/index.html) and
   [日吉](https://www.students.keio.ac.jp/hy/class/registration/index.html).
   Follow the applicable cohort's handbook, not just the newest publication year.
3. Check the degree/track, applicable entrants, category minima, exclusions, credit-recognition caps,
   required subjects, residence period, and thesis/project/examination conditions. Early completion,
   double degrees, transfers, and professional programs can have separate conditions.
4. Cite the exact official page or handbook section and its cohort. If a rule is unavailable publicly,
   requires an unprovided cohort/track, or remains ambiguous, state that it is unverified and skip that
   conclusion. Do not substitute another program's rule. Use an already known context before asking.
5. When comparing with a requested record read, separate earned credits from registered/expected credits
   and identify which category mappings remain unverified. A total-credit subtraction alone cannot
   establish eligibility. Return the school's displayed `Determination` separately from any comparison.

Done when the source and its applicability are identified, or the unanswered part is explicitly marked.

## Unverified and instructor-specific rules

- Other faculties, graduate schools, cohorts and special tracks remain supported through official-source
  lookup; no numeric graduation default is supplied for them. These rows are not Hatch choices.
- No university-wide GPA conversion, progression threshold, attendance limit, or graduation-credit total
  has been established here. Check the applicable handbook. For attendance, lateness, assessment weights,
  and instructor-specific conditions, check the exact course's syllabus and official course announcements;
  do not infer a common absence allowance from another course.
- A user-supplied requirement is a lead to verify, not a verified pack rule. Keep individual academic
  circumstances and account values out of this reference.
